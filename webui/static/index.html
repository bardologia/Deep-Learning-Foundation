A/B autopsy

A guided comparison of two saved inferences over the same region: the largest relative metric gaps with the winner per row, and the locations where the runs disagree most, each with both predicted profiles against the ground truth.

Error triage

The worst regions of a saved inference, ranked by error and tagged with their dominant failure mode, label quality, and seed disagreement. Open any case in the cube explorer, then record a verdict: label problem, model problem, or interesting. Annotations persist under logs/triage/ and build the qualitative case library.

Model microscope

Load a trained backbone or dual checkpoint, pick a pixel on the scene, and inspect the model from the inside: the predicted profile against ground truth and the raw cube, gradient attribution of every output family to every input channel, spatial maps of where in the window those gradients concentrate, feature maps along a clickable view of the architecture, and live what-if perturbations of the input.

Model survey

The microscope's diagnostics, run over every pixel of the test region instead of one. Pick a trained run and the model is re-run tile by tile: prediction fit and matched scatterer errors, channel attribution and ablation, flip symmetry, noise robustness, occlusion evidence distance, and layer vitals, all averaged over the whole region with a progress bar while it works.

Sweep DataLoader settings against the real data path and find the configuration that keeps the GPU saturated. Each candidate is measured three ways — pure loader throughput, the GPU compute ceiling, and end-to-end with the data-wait fraction — so starvation is quantified, not guessed.
