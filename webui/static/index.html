Results

A/B autopsy

A guided comparison of two saved inferences over the same region: the largest relative metric gaps with the winner per row, and the locations where the runs disagree most, each with both predictions of every target channel against the target.

Model microscope

Load a trained backbone checkpoint, pick a pixel on the map, and inspect the model from the inside: the prediction of every target channel against its target, gradient attribution of every target channel to every input channel, spatial maps of where in the window those gradients concentrate, channel ablation, occlusion and flip symmetry, and feature maps along a clickable view of the architecture.

Model survey

The microscope's diagnostics, run over every pixel of the test region instead of one. Pick a trained run and the model is re-run tile by tile: prediction fit per target channel, channel attribution and ablation, flip symmetry, noise robustness, occlusion evidence distance, and layer vitals, all averaged over the whole region with a progress bar while it works.
